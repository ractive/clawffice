# Dunder Mifflin Scranton Branch — Floor Plan Reference

The office is on the 2nd floor of Scranton Business Park (1725 Slough Ave). Warehouse is below.

## Overall Layout

Roughly rectangular. Main entrance on the left, windows along the top (north). Two zones connected by hallway past the kitchen: **Main Office** (west) and **Annex** (east).

## Areas

### 1. Entrance / Reception (left/west)
- Main door from elevator lobby → waiting area (couch, chairs)
- **Pam's reception desk** — curved, faces entrance, can see entire bullpen
- Dunder Mifflin logo on wall behind reception

### 2. Michael's Office (front-left corner)
- Private corner office, windows on two walls
- Adjacent to reception, glass windows/blinds into bullpen
- Desk, guest chairs, small couch area
- "World's Best Boss" mug, Dundies, movie posters

### 3. Conference Room (front, next to Michael's office)
- Along north wall, glass walls into bullpen
- Long rectangular table, whiteboard, TV on rolling cart

### 4. Bullpen / Open Desks (center)
Desks in clusters of 2-4 facing each other:

**Sales cluster (near Michael's office):**
- Dwight — closest to Michael's door
- Jim — directly next to/across from Dwight
- Andy — near Jim/Dwight cluster

**Sales cluster (center-right):**
- Stanley — facing away from action
- Phyllis — cluster with Stanley

**Back of bullpen:**
- Meredith — along back wall
- Creed — opposite Meredith

### 5. Accounting (far right of main office)
- Three desks clustered: Angela, Oscar, Kevin
- Near hallway to kitchen/annex

### 6. Kitchen / Break Room (center-right, transition zone)
- Fridge, microwave, sink, counter, coffee maker, small table
- Vending machines (2)

### 7. Hallway / Corridor
- Restrooms, coat rack, water cooler, copier/printer, supply closet

### 8. The Annex (east, beyond break room)
- Isolated lower-status workspace
- Toby (HR), Kelly (Customer Service), Ryan (temp/various)
- Cubicle partitions, shelving

## Game Map Notes
- Suggested grid: ~60-80 tiles wide × 40-50 tiles tall (16px tiles = 960-1280 × 640-800)
- Pam at reception has sight lines to most of bullpen
- Annex should feel disconnected (hallway separation)
- Floor: commercial carpet (gray/blue), kitchen has tile
- Fluorescent overhead lighting
